# Lead Magnet: The Postcode Job Report

A personalised report, built by hand for one trade business in one area. It shows how many local people searched for their trade last month, who got those jobs, and the exact fixes to win more of them.

**Why it beats a PDF:** Every agency has a free guide. Almost none give away a custom audit, because they sell it. This one is about *their* postcode, *their* competitors and *their* Google listing. Hormozi: give away the "what" and "why" for free, and sell the "do it for me."

**Defaults used (change any of them):**
- **Who it's for:** UK roofers first. The same template works for electricians, plumbers and kitchen fitters.
- **The one pain:** "I don't know where my next job is coming from."
- **Next step:** A 5-minute Loom walking through their report, then "Want me to set this up for you?"
- **Format:** Calculator / audit hybrid. Personalised, so it converts hardest.

## 4. How to build one in 30 minutes

All free tools.

1. **Searches and costs (10 min):** Google Ads Keyword Planner. Set the location to their postcodes. Pull the 8 to 10 searches with the most buyers. Copy the monthly searches and the "top of page bid" range.
2. **Competitors (5 min):** Search "roofer [town]" in Google Maps. Note the top 3, their reviews and ratings. Check if they run ads in the Google Ads Transparency Center.
3. **Their Google listing (5 min):** Score it against the 10 points.
4. **Their website (5 min):** Open it on your phone. Run Google PageSpeed Insights. Score it against the 5 points.
5. **Plan and Loom (5 min):** Pick the 3 free fixes with the biggest gaps. Record a 5-minute Loom scrolling through the report.

**Cap it at [5] a week.** It's built by hand, so the limit is real. Say so in your content.

## 8. Self-audit (7 traits)

| Trait | Pass? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Solves one problem | ✅ | "Where's my next job coming from?" |
| 2. One ICP, named in title | ✅ | "Roofer" and their town are in the headline |
| 3. Outcome + time + objection | ⚠️ | Promises a real number in 48 hours. The objection ("I don't have time for marketing") is only handled on page 3. |
| 4. Gives away 80%+ | ✅ | Full demand data, competitors, scores and 3 free fixes |
| 5. Read in 5 to 10 minutes | ✅ | 3 pages plus a 5-minute Loom |
| 6. Low-friction next step | ✅ | Reply YES, then a call |
| 7. Clean, not over-designed | ⚠️ | Needs a Canva template with your branding so each report is a copy-and-fill job |

**Risks to know:**
- **It costs your time.** 30 minutes each × 5 a week = 2.5 hours. Only build reports for people who pass the form, and prioritise higher job values.
- **Keyword Planner shows wide ranges** unless the Google Ads account has spend running. Your agency account should show exact numbers.
- **Small towns can show low search numbers.** Widen the area to the full service radius before sending.
- **Ask Mike** before using his name and quote publicly.
