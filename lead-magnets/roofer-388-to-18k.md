### PAGE 2: The exact system (copy it)

**Step 1. Target people who need a roofer today.**
Run Google Ads on two kinds of search only:
- **Emergency:** "emergency roof repair [your postcode]"
- **Planned:** "new roof quote [your postcode]"

*Why it works:* Nobody types that unless they're ready to pay someone. You're not convincing anyone. You're just showing up when they're looking.
*Do it yourself:* Set the ad location to the postcodes you actually cover. Block searches like "jobs", "course", "DIY" and "salary" so you don't pay for clicks from people who'll never hire you.

**Step 2. Send clicks to one page, not your homepage.**
One page. One job. Get them to ask for a callback.
- Your phone number at the top
- **A same-day callback offer:** "Request a callback and we'll ring you today."
- A short form: name, postcode, what's wrong with the roof
- 3 photos of your real work and one real review

*Why it works:* A homepage gives people 10 things to click. This page gives them one.

**Step 3. Ring back in minutes, not days.**
Most roofers call back the next evening. By then the homeowner has three other quotes lined up.
Set the form to text your phone the second someone fills it in. Ring straight away. If you're on a roof, ring at your next break.

**Step 4. Book the survey on WhatsApp within 24 hours.**
If they don't answer, don't wait. Message them:

> **Message 1 (straight after the missed call):**
> "Hi [Name], it's [You] from [Company]. Just tried ringing about your roof. When's good for a quick look? I've got [day] or [day] free."

> **Message 2 (4 hours later):**
> "Hi [Name], is the leak getting worse? If it's urgent I can try to get someone out today."

> **Message 3 (next morning):**
> "Morning [Name], still keen to get the roof sorted? Just reply with a day and I'll book you in."

*Why it works:* People ignore calls. They read WhatsApp.

**Step 5. Track three numbers every week.**
Calls. Quotes. Jobs won. A notes app is fine. If calls are low, fix the ads. If quotes are low, fix your callback speed. If jobs are low, fix your quote.
