Questions people ask us

Before you book

Do I have to pay anything upfront?

No. We don't take deposits on roof restorations. You inspect the work first, and you only pay when you're happy.

Will you make a mess of my garden?

No. We cover your lawn and paths before we start. At the end we clear all the moss and debris and leave the area clean.

What if I've already got a cheaper quote?

Send it to us. We'll beat any genuine like-for-like quote. We may ask to see it.

What if you find broken tiles?

Every package includes free minor repairs, so we replace missing or broken tiles while we're up there.

Do you do bigger repairs and new roofs?

Yes. We do roof repairs, chimney repairs, flat roofs and full new roofs. Ask for a free quote.

How fast can I get a price?

Within 24 hours. Add a photo of your roof to the form and we can often price it straight away.
