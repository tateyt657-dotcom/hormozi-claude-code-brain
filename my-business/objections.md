## Facts that answer each one (draft, not yet tested on calls)

These are Powell's own facts lined up against each objection. Turn them into call scripts with the Closing playbook.

| Objection | Facts to use |
|---|---|
| Price | The £1,200 monthly fee doesn't start until the completed-jobs target is hit, and the 90-day guarantee means Powell works free until then. Setup is £750 against £1,250 normally. Proof: a Portsmouth roofer turned £388 of ad spend into £18,000 of completed jobs. |
| "How are you different?" | Roofer first, and only one trade per area. Paid on completed jobs, not leads or clicks. | Timing | Only one trade per area, so a competitor can take the spot. Founding rate. Idle crew and wages cost money every quiet week. |
