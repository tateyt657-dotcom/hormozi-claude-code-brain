# Objections

## Likely objections and current answers (Inferred)

These come from the arguments the reels already make. Replace them with real objections from sales calls as they come up.

| Objection | Answer used in Powell's content |
|---|---|
| "I already have a website." | The website doesn't matter if the phone goes to voicemail. Fix the phone first. |
| "I'll just ring them back later." | By then they've called the next trade. The first trade to reply usually gets the job. |
| "I get enough work from word of mouth." | New customers check Google first. They call the trade with 60 reviews, not 4. |
| "I don't want to pester customers for reviews." | Ask every customer on the day, while they're looking at the job, and text the link. No chasing. |
| "Automated texts feel fake." | The text comes from your number and sounds like you ("Sorry, up a roof. What's the job?"). You still ring them back. |
