Source: `reels/` on branch `claude/affectionate-ramanujan-yvyse8` in `tateyt657-dotcom/powell-marketing-content`.

Four talking-head reels for Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, each about 27 seconds.

| # | Reel | Hook | CTA keyword |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Missed calls | "A roofer I know lost a job last week because he was up a ladder." | TEXT |
| 2 | Speed to lead | "Most trades businesses take 6 hours to reply to a form enquiry." | FAST |
| 3 | Google reviews | "Nobody calls the roofer with 4 reviews." | LINK |
| 4 | Hard truth | "Your website isn't bringing you work. Your phone is." | none (sign-off) |

- **Posting order:** 4, 1, 2, 3, one a day.
- **Sign-off line:** "Go get what you deserve."
- **How to judge a winner:** keyword comments and enquiries at 48 hours, not views.
- **Voice:** blunt, UK trades language, short sentences, no "Hey guys" openers.
