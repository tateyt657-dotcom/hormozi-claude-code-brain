## What the reply threads showed (Nov 2026 review)

Source: "Instantly positive replies: roster and outcomes" (125 leads, Oct 2025 to Nov 2026).

- 12 leads asked for a call. Only 3 got a time. The rest got a video or qualifying questions first.
- At least 17 leads were promised a video, and no link appears in the thread. Some got "software errors" or "my setter" excuses, and two got a placeholder ("THE VIDEO") instead of a link.
- The roofer Loom gave DIY Google ranking tips and didn't sell the Google Ads offer.
- 13 leads asked the price and got at least 7 different answers (£250, £299, £750+, £750 to £2,000/month, £1,250/month, deposit + monthly, 5%).
- Leads who replied stayed in automated sequences and got cold emails afterwards.

## How replies work now

Charlie is on roofs from 7am to 4pm. So:

- **During the day:** the video does the selling. One pre-recorded roofer video covers the Google Ads offer, the 90-day guarantee and the £2,500 to £57,780 result. On breaks, reply from the phone with a saved template: the video link and two evening call times.
- **Call slots:** weekday evenings from 5pm. No Saturdays.
- **"Call me":** reply with an evening time the same day, then ring.
- **"How much?":** one answer, the core offer price. No other prices.
- **Once a lead replies:** remove them from every other campaign.
- **Offer is locked:** no new prices, angles or offers. Run the numbers as they are.
