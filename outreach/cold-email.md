# Cold Email: Trades Outreach

**Funnel:** Cold email → they reply → Postcode Job Report + Loom → booked call

**The angle:** Don't ask strangers for a call. Show them one true fact about their business, then offer the report for free. The Lead Nurture playbook gives this exact move as its example of personalised outreach:

> *"Hey Greg, I see that your site traffic is down 18% this quarter, we had another customer in a similar situation. They're now up 100% 90 days later. I prepped a walkthrough to show you how we can apply the same principles to your auto shop."*

---

## 1. Who to email

**Target:** UK roofing companies that are **limited companies** (see the law section below).

**Good fit:**
- Has a Google listing and some reviews (an established business)
- **Not** in the top 3 on Google Maps for "roofer [town]"
- **Not** running Google Ads (check the Google Ads Transparency Center)
- Has a website or a Google listing with an email address

These businesses do good work but aren't getting found. Your fix is built for exactly them.

**Where to get the list:**
1. Scrape Google Maps for "roofer" across your target towns (tools: Outscraper, Apify).
2. Check each one on Companies House. Keep the **Ltd** companies.
3. Find the owner's name and email (website, Companies House director name, an email finder).
4. Note one personal detail per lead: their map position, review count, or a gap in their listing.

---

## 2. The law (read this first)

UK marketing email rules (PECR) treat businesses differently:

- **Limited companies and LLPs:** you can cold email them. Say who you are and give an easy way to opt out.
- **Sole traders and most partnerships:** count as individuals. You need their **consent before** sending marketing email.

Lots of roofers are sole traders. **Only cold email limited companies.** Ring the sole traders instead.

Every email needs your name, your business and a one-line opt-out. Remove anyone who says no, straight away and for good.

*This is a summary, not legal advice. Check the ICO's guidance on business-to-business marketing before you launch.*

---

## 3. Set-up (so you land in the inbox, not spam)

1. **Never send from your main domain.** Buy 2 to 3 similar domains (e.g. powellmarketing.co, trypowellmarketing.co.uk) and point them to your real site.
2. **2 inboxes per domain**, each with a real name: charlie@...
3. **Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC** on every domain. Your email provider has a guide.
4. **Warm up each inbox for 2 to 3 weeks** before sending (Instantly and Smartlead do this for you).
5. **Keep volume low:** start around 20 a day per inbox, rising to about 30 to 50. This is a common rule of thumb, not a hard limit.
6. **Plain text only.** No images, no links and no tracking in email 1.

Example: 3 domains × 2 inboxes × 30 a day = **180 emails a day**.

## 4. The sequence (4 emails, 12 days)

Keep every email under 90 words. Emails 2 to 4 reply in the same thread.

### Email 1 (Day 0)

**Subject:** roofer searches in [town]

> Hi [First name],
>
> I searched "roofer [town]" on Google Maps this morning. [Competitor 1] and [Competitor 2] come up first. [Business] wasn't in the top [10].
>
> About [X] people in your area searched for a roofer last month.
>
> I'm a roofer myself, and I get trades booked jobs from Google. I've put together a short report on who's winning those searches, with 3 free fixes for your listing.
>
> Want me to send it over?
>
> Charlie
> Powell Marketing UK
>
> *Not for you? Reply "no" and I won't email again.*

### Email 2 (Day 3)

> Hi [First name],
>
> Quick one. A 4-man roofing crew in Portsmouth spent £388 on Google Ads. 15 calls. 13 quotes. 4 re-roofs. £18,000 in 60 days.
>
> The same setup would work in [town]. Want the report?
>
> Charlie

### Email 3 (Day 7)

> Hi [First name],
>
> Most roofers I talk to have been burned by an agency before. So here's how I work: we agree a booked-job target, and if you don't hit it in 60 days, you don't pay. No contract.
>
> Want me to record you a 5-minute video showing how it would work in [town]?
>
> Charlie

### Email 4 (Day 12)

> Hi [First name],
>
> I'll leave it here. If you ever want the [town] numbers, just reply "report" and I'll send them.
>
> Charlie

## 5. Add phone calls

Trades answer phones more than email. The Lead Nurture playbook says to *start* on every channel, then carry on wherever they reply, and to reach out 5+ times instead of giving up after 1.

- **Day 1:** Ring once. "I emailed you yesterday about the roofing searches in [town]. Did it come through?"
- **Day 8:** Ring once more after email 3.
- Try early morning or after 5pm, before or after they're on a roof.

Sole traders get the phone calls only, no email.

---

## 6. When they reply "yes"

1. **Within the hour:** "Great, I'll have it to you within 48 hours. What's the best mobile for WhatsApp?"
2. Build their **Postcode Job Report** (`lead-magnets/postcode-job-report.md`, 30 minutes).
3. Send it with a **5-minute Loom** walking through it.
4. **Ask:** "Want me to set up fixes 4 and 5 for you? I'll show you how it'd work on a quick call. [two times]"
5. No reply? Follow the "Got the Loom, didn't book" steps on the funnel map.

**Replies like "how much?" or "who are you?"** count as interest. Answer in one line, then offer the report.

---

## 7. Test and track

Track per 100 emails sent: **replies, "yes" replies, reports sent, calls booked.**

Apply the Hooks playbook's 70-20-10 rule to subject lines and first lines. Run 70% on your best performer, 20% on twists of it and 10% on new ideas. Change one thing at a time so you know what moved the numbers.

**Subject lines to test:**
- roofer searches in [town]
- [Business] on google maps
- [X] people searched for a roofer in [town]
- quick question about [Business]
