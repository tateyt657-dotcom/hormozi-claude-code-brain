Don't leave it too late

Damp doesn't fix itself. The longer it sits, the bigger the job and the bill.

  • Mould spreads

    Spores move to clothes, furniture and other rooms.

  • Timber rots

    Wet joists and floorboards go soft and have to be replaced.

  • Plaster fails

    Salts from rising damp break up plaster, so it has to come off.

  • Heat escapes

    Damp walls lose heat faster, so your home costs more to warm.

  • Your health suffers

    The NHS warns damp and mould can cause breathing problems and asthma.

  • Landlords face deadlines

    Awaab's Law puts new duties on landlords to act on damp and mould quickly.
