Don't leave it too late

Mould and damp don't go away on their own. The longer they're left, the further they spread: into your home, your health and your bank balance.

  1. Caught early

    A patch, a smell, a few black spots

    The fix: find the cause, treat it and make good.

    Typical cost: confirm with Elliot

  2. Left a while

    Mould spreads and plaster fails

    The fix: strip and replaster walls, replace rotten skirting and treat every room it reached.

    Typical cost: confirm with Elliot

  3. Left for years

    Rot gets into joists and floors

    The fix: lift floors, replace joists, sometimes a full refurbishment. See the Bournemouth floor and the Christchurch bungalow.

    Typical cost: confirm with Elliot

Your health

  • Mould releases spores you breathe in, often in the bedroom where you sleep.
  • The NHS warns it can cause breathing problems and make asthma and allergies worse.
  • Babies, children, older people and anyone with a lung condition are most at risk.

Your home

  • Wet timber rots. Dry rot can even spread through brickwork to reach new wood.
  • Salts from rising damp keep drawing in moisture, so the plaster has to come off.
  • Mould stains paint, wallpaper, carpets, clothes and furniture.

Your money

  • A small treatment turns into new plaster, new floors and new joists.
  • Damp walls lose heat faster, so your heating bills go up.
  • Damp found in a buyer's survey can mean a lower offer or a lost sale.
  • Many home insurance policies won't pay for damage that builds up slowly, like damp and rot.
